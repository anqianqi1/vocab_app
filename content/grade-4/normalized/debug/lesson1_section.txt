                  EXERCISE C: Extend Your Vocabulary
                  The adverb suffix -ly
                  The key word gradually ends in the suffix -ly. Gradually tells how something is
                  done. Many other words that end in -ly also tell how something is done. Examples:
                  quickly, neatly, carefully, bravely. These words are called adverbs.
